# Assumptions

The User Journeys document describes some features that, in a real product,
would rely on heavy external integrations or specialised libraries. To keep the
project focused on a complete and demonstrable core flow within the milestone
scope, we made the following reasonable assumptions and simplifications, as
permitted by the brief ("Any missing details may be reasonably assumed").

1. **Authentication & accounts.** Login and registration use JWT tokens.
   Organizers can create stakeholder accounts; created accounts get a default
   password (`changeme123`). Self-registration is open to Vendors, Guests and
   Venue Owners. Guests are linked to their invitations by email address.

2. **Invitations.** "Sending" an invitation marks the guest as invited in the
   platform rather than dispatching a real email. Guests then see the
   invitation — with full event details — on their **My Invitations** page,
   where they RSVP and set dietary preferences. This keeps the whole journey
   demonstrable inside the app without an email provider.

3. **Day-of communications.** Messages are delivered in-app: a receipt row is
   created per guest, guests view messages on their Invitations page and mark
   them as seen, and organizers can send a follow-up that goes **only** to
   guests who have not yet seen the original. Real push/SMS infrastructure is
   out of scope; the seed data pre-marks some receipts as seen so the
   seen-count demo works immediately.

4. **Guest check-in.** Check-in is performed by staff/organizers via a button,
   representing the "scan QR / confirm name" step. Physical QR scanning is not
   implemented.

5. **Venue layout / floor-plan designer.** The drag-and-drop floor plan
   designer and image/PDF export of layouts were considered out of scope (they
   require a dedicated canvas library). The rest of the planning flow is fully
   implemented.

6. **Venue availability calendar & photos.** Owners manage availability by
   approving/declining booking requests rather than a separate calendar UI;
   photo upload is represented by a text field in the data model.

7. **Invoices.** "Attach supporting documents" is simplified to an itemized
   text details field; status notifications are visible in-app as live status
   changes (Pending Review → Approved → Paid) rather than emails.

8. **Reports export.** Event reports export as a downloadable text summary
   covering costs, attendance and outcomes, satisfying "generate and export"
   without a PDF rendering dependency. Venue-owner performance reports are
   shown on the owner dashboard.

9. **Currency & locale.** Money values are shown in EGP, consistent with the
   Cairo-based examples in the brief.

10. **Database.** A single SQLite file (`backend/popeyez.db`) is generated by
    `npm run seed` and intentionally not committed — the seed script is the
    reproducible source of dummy data, as recommended by the guidelines.
